Privacy Policy

One Verse Today (今日經句) · Effective November 13, 2026

One Verse Today shows one Bible verse a day. It is built to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved (favorite) verses
  • Your daily reminder setting and time
  • Your chosen Bible translation

These are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone; no server is involved.

Sharing

When you choose to share a verse, the app hands the text, image, or link to your phone's share sheet. What happens next is up to the app you share to. Shared links open a simple web page that shows the verse; that page does not use cookies or trackers.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/WayneChen983/one-verse-today.

隱私權政策

今日經句（One Verse Today）· 2026 年 11 月 13 日生效

今日經句每天為你準備一節經文，所有功能都在你的裝置上完成。

我們蒐集什麼

什麼都不蒐集。本 App 沒有帳號、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也沒有任何第三方 SDK，不會把任何個人資料傳給我們或其他人。

只留在你裝置上的資料

  • 你收藏的經文
  • 每日提醒的開關與時間
  • 你選擇的經文譯本

這些資料只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 時一併移除。每日提醒是手機排定的本地通知，不經過任何伺服器。

分享

當你分享經文時，App 只是把文字、圖片或連結交給手機的分享功能，之後如何處理由你選擇的 App 決定。分享連結開啟的網頁只顯示經文，不使用 Cookie 或追蹤工具。

兒童

本 App 不蒐集任何人的資料，包括兒童。

變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新本頁與上方日期。

聯絡我們

有任何問題，請到 github.com/WayneChen983/one-verse-today 留言。
